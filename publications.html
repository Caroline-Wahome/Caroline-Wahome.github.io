RESEARCH OUTPUT

Research & Publications

Research exploring microorganisms, agricultural systems, soil health and sustainable approaches to environmental and agricultural challenges.

From Microbial Ecology to Sustainable Systems

My publications bring together environmental microbiology, microbial ecology, soil health and sustainable agriculture. Across my research, I explore how microorganisms, agricultural systems and accessible technologies can contribute to healthier soils, resilient production systems and sustainable development.

Research Themes

Environmental Microbiology

Microbial communities, environmental health and antibiotic-resistant bacterial pathogens.

Soil & Microbial Ecology

Bacterial and fungal communities associated with agricultural soils and plant production systems.

Sustainable Agriculture

Sustainable crop production, smallholder farming systems and agricultural technology adoption.

Plant–Microbe Interactions

Beneficial microorganisms, arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi and plant establishment.

Research Trajectory

My research has evolved from environmental microbiology and microbial quality assessment toward plant–microbe interactions, soil microbial ecology and sustainable agricultural systems, and increasingly toward accessible environmental monitoring and interdisciplinary innovation.
