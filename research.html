RESEARCH IN PRACTICE

From the Laboratory to the Field

These images capture different stages of my research — from plant–microbe experiments and laboratory testing to sensor fabrication and soil sampling.

PLANT–MICROBE INTERACTIONS

Helping Banana Plantlets Establish

My doctoral research investigated the use of arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi to support the acclimatization of tissue-culture banana plantlets.

Moving plants from controlled tissue-culture conditions into the growing environment is a critical stage of micropropagation, where plant losses can be substantial. Understanding beneficial plant–microbe interactions can help improve establishment and survival.

ENVIRONMENTAL BIOSENSING

Bringing Microbiology into Sensor Development

My current research extends my environmental microbiology background into the development of accessible approaches for soil monitoring.

Working across microbiology, soil science, engineering and technology creates opportunities to translate biological knowledge into practical environmental sensing tools.

SENSOR TESTING

Testing the Science Behind the Sensor

Sensor development involves iterative testing, troubleshooting and measurement to understand how the electrode system responds.

Simple laboratory measurements such as conductivity testing form an important part of evaluating whether a developing sensing approach can generate useful and reliable information.

MATERIALS & FABRICATION

Where Science Meets Design

Developing accessible sensors also requires experimentation with materials and fabrication techniques.

Here, a linseed-oil layer is applied as part of the fabrication process, illustrating how biological questions can lead into materials science, design and engineering.

SENSOR FABRICATION

Turning an Idea into an Electrode

Following preparation and curing of the substrate, carbon paste electrodes can be printed onto the sensing surface.

This stage represents the practical transition from a research concept towards a physical platform that can eventually be tested for environmental applications.

SOIL & ENVIRONMENTAL MONITORING

Soil Is Where the Story Begins

Ultimately, soil monitoring is about understanding the environment in which plants grow and agricultural systems function.

Soil sampling connects laboratory research with the realities of agricultural landscapes and provides the starting point for generating information that can support better soil management.
