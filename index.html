About Me

I am a scientist and researcher working across environmental and agricultural systems with a focus on developing sustainable approaches that contribute to healthy soils, resilient agricultural systems and environmental sustainability.


My research interests span soil health, environmental microbiology, phytoremediation, climate-smart agriculture, waste valorisation and sustainable management of natural resources.

Research Expertise

🌱 Soil Health

Research on soil quality, soil biodiversity, nutrient cycling and sustainable soil management.

🌿 Phytoremediation

Exploring plant-based approaches for remediation of contaminated soils and environmental restoration.

🌍 Climate-Smart Agriculture

Developing sustainable agricultural approaches that enhance resilience to climate change.

🦠 Environmental Microbiology

Investigating microorganisms and their roles in environmental processes and ecosystem health.

♻️ Waste Valorisation

Exploring approaches for converting organic waste into valuable resources for agriculture and environmental sustainability.

🐛 Soil Biodiversity

Understanding soil organisms and their contributions to ecosystem functioning and soil health.
