About Me

I am a microbiologist and researcher with over 10 years of experience delivering multidisciplinary research, technical advisory, stakeholder engagement and capacity-building initiatives across climate-smart agriculture, environmental sustainability, natural resource management and environmental monitoring.


My work brings together scientific research, stakeholder engagement and practical innovation to support sustainable and climate-resilient land-use solutions. I have worked with academic institutions, farming communities, development partners and cross-sector stakeholders in Kenya and internationally.


I am particularly interested in translating scientific evidence into practical interventions that contribute to sustainable development, strengthen research impact and support climate-positive innovation.

Research Areas

My research is multidisciplinary and focuses on understanding environmental and agricultural systems while developing practical approaches for sustainable and climate-resilient development.

Environmental Microbiology

Investigating microorganisms and microbial communities within environmental and agricultural systems and their implications for ecosystem functioning and sustainability.

Soil Health & Environmental Monitoring

Developing accessible approaches for soil monitoring and environmental assessment, including the development of zero-waste biosensors for environmental monitoring in soils.

Climate-Smart Agriculture

Researching sustainable agricultural approaches that strengthen resilience, support smallholder farmers and contribute to climate-positive land-use systems.

Sustainable Agricultural Systems

Exploring beneficial microorganisms, sustainable crop production, low-cost technologies and approaches that improve agricultural productivity and sustainability.

Natural Resource Management

Supporting sustainable management of land and natural resources through interdisciplinary research and stakeholder-driven approaches.

Science, Innovation & Public Engagement

Connecting scientific research with communities, policymakers and wider audiences through engagement, capacity building and accessible science communication.
