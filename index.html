ABOUT CAROLINE

From Microbiology to Sustainable Systems

I am an Environmental Microbiologist and Researcher interested in how microorganisms and biological processes shape environmental and agricultural systems.

My research journey has developed across microbial ecology, soil health, plant-associated microorganisms, sustainable agriculture and environmental monitoring. More recently, my work has expanded into innovative approaches for making environmental monitoring more accessible and useful to farmers and communities.

I work across laboratory research, field-based activities, interdisciplinary collaboration and stakeholder engagement, with a particular interest in translating scientific knowledge into practical solutions.
