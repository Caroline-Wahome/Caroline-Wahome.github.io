About Me

I am a microbiologist and researcher with over 10 years of experience delivering multidisciplinary research, technical advisory, stakeholder engagement and capacity-building initiatives across environmental sustainability, climate-smart agriculture, natural resource management and environmental monitoring.


My work brings together environmental microbiology, agricultural systems, environmental monitoring and stakeholder engagement to develop practical approaches for sustainable and climate-resilient development.


I have worked with academic institutions, farming communities, development partners and cross-sector stakeholders in Kenya and internationally. I am particularly interested in translating scientific evidence into practical interventions that support sustainable development and climate-positive innovation.

Research

My research brings together microbiology, environmental monitoring, agricultural systems and sustainability to address challenges affecting soils, crops, farming communities and environmental resilience.

Environmental Microbiology

Investigating microorganisms and microbial communities within environmental and agricultural systems and exploring their roles in ecosystem functioning, sustainability and environmental health.

Soil Health & Environmental Monitoring

Developing accessible approaches for soil monitoring and environmental assessment, including zero-waste biosensor technologies for environmental monitoring in soils.

Sustainable Agriculture

Researching sustainable agricultural practices, beneficial microorganisms, crop production systems and approaches that support productive and resilient farming systems.

Climate-Smart Agriculture

Exploring approaches that strengthen agricultural resilience, support smallholder farmers and contribute to sustainable and climate-positive land-use systems.

Beneficial Microorganisms & Crop Systems

Research on beneficial microorganisms, arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi, tissue culture technologies and microbial communities associated with agricultural production systems.

Science, Innovation & Public Engagement

Connecting scientific research with farmers, communities, institutions and wider audiences through stakeholder engagement, capacity building and accessible science communication.
